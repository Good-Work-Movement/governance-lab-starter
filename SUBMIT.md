# Plain submission prompts

Use these prompts if JSON is inconvenient. Keep the report short enough to inspect.

- Task ID and submission title:
- Contributor handle or `anonymous`:
- Optional operator/maintainer handle for AI-assisted work:
- What did you do, and what exact input did you use?
- Expected result or rule under examination:
- Observed result, including a negative result or failure:
- Who may be affected, absent, or downstream?
- Evidence and source locators (or `none`):
- What is fact, inference, proposal, or unresolved?
- AI/tool/model provenance, if used; otherwise `none` or `unknown`:
- Attribution preference and reuse preference:
- May reviewers contact you about this report? `yes` / `no` / `only through handle`
- Anything to withdraw or redact before review?
