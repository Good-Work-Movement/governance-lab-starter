# Small task board

All times are estimates. Stop whenever you need to. A good counterexample or a clear objection is useful even when it does not produce code.

| ID | Time | Input | Deliverable | Review asks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CONTRIB-START-001 | 15 min | `examples/water-case.json` | Answers to the six water questions and one added assumption | Is the harm concrete? Are affected, absent, and downstream parties named? |
| CONTRIB-START-002 | 30–60 min | Water case plus your chosen AI assistant | AI reasoning, one minimal adversarial variant, operator/model provenance | Which claims are independently checked? What did the model miss or invent? |
| CONTRIB-START-003 | Future design | This packet | Plain-language objection to the mission, task, source reading, or manager | Requires a later release record and review route; not an accepted task ID in this offline starter bundle. |
| CONTRIB-START-004 | Future release | A released fixture or procedure | Independent reproduction with commands, environment, output, and failures | Requires a later released fixture; not an accepted task ID in this offline starter bundle. |

Tasks 001 and 002 are the currently supported offline starter tasks; 001 is recommended first. A contributor may propose a different bounded question, but it needs a later task record before submission can be accepted against this packet.
